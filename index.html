One loop, run continuously

Most catalog tools clean a dataset once and stop. Claro keeps resolving, validating and enriching every time a feed lands or a record drifts — so the catalog gets more accurate the longer it runs.

01

Ingest from any source

Connect ERPs, PIMs, supplier portals, spreadsheets and PDFs. No migration, no fixed schema required upfront.

02

Map, match, classify

Align schemas, deduplicate records, and resolve the same product across suppliers into one stable ID.

03

Fill gaps with confidence

Missing attributes get enriched from approved sources, each value carrying a confidence score and its origin.

04

Write back, continuously

Approved records flow into ERP, PIM and search — and Claro keeps watching for drift after that.
